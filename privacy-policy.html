Privacy Policy

Fitment Scanner is an internal tool used to look up OEM part fitment data and create draft listings on eBay. It is not a public consumer application.

What data this app accesses

This app connects to eBay's Sell APIs solely to create draft inventory listings on the operator's own eBay seller account. It does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to eBay buyers or other third parties.

Data storage

Part numbers and fitment data entered or scanned through this tool are used only to generate listing drafts and are not shared with any party other than eBay, as part of creating those drafts.

Contact

For questions about this tool, contact the site operator directly.
